How it actually works

Three honest steps.
No syllabus required.

01 — LIST

Add what you can teach, add what you want to learn

Two lists, that's it. A skill you're confident enough to explain, and a skill you've been meaning to pick up.

02 — MATCH

We surface people whose lists complete yours

Our matching looks for the overlap — someone who wants what you teach, and teaches what you want.

03 — SWAP & EARN

Teach a session, earn credits. Spend them to learn.

No cash changes hands. Teaching fills your balance, learning draws it down — everyone stays in the loop.

The credit loop

One currency: your time.

Credits aren't points for a leaderboard — they're a running record of who's shown up for whom.

+1

You teach a session

Run a 45–60 min session on something you know. Your balance goes up.

↻

Credits sit in your balance

No expiry, no tiers. One credit roughly equals one hour of someone's attention.

−1

You learn a session

Book time with someone teaching what you want. Your balance goes down.
